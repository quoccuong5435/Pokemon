# Plan cai tien Pokemon Arena (de duyet)

Hien co: 162 Pokemon playable, 4 chieu Q W E R + Ability, TM/HM 3000 vang, 8 gym doi 6 con, legend (room + spawn ngoai), Soul,
Mega (25), item he (3000), Poke Center, chicken.

## A. Dinh huong choi (chon 1)
1. **Hanh trinh Champion (PvE)**: lam 8 gym -> huy hieu -> mo Elite Four + Champion (boss cuoi). Legend la boss phu / dung de manh len.
2. **Dau truong (PvP)**: ban do chia doi, dua tai nguyen + duel; gym/legend la muc tieu co buff cho ca doi.
3. **Lai (de nhat, nen chon)**: PvE co huy hieu lam dich, PvP bang duel tu nguyen (xep hang, cuoc vang).

## B. Item
- **Held item theo he/tien hoa** (Leftovers, Choice Band/Specs, Life Orb, Focus Sash, Berry hoi mau): 1 o held, anh huong chieu.
- **Item tien hoa**: Fire/Water/Thunder/Leaf/Moon Stone, Link Cable (tien hoa trade), Everstone (chan tien hoa) - hien tien hoa moi chi theo level.
- **Huy hieu gym**: moi huy hieu +cap toi da (level cap 40/55/70/...) hoac + chi so, buoc phai co du huy hieu de vao Elite Four.
- **Item tieu hao**: Potion/Revive/Rare Candy (hiem), X-Attack... mua o Poke Mart theo gym da qua.
- **Mega Stone / Z-Crystal**: giu Mega, them Z-move 1 lan/tran cho 18 he.
- **Nang cap**: ban item rac -> doi Rare Candy / Soul fragment.

## C. Duel
- Lenh `-duel <ten>` / bam NPC Duel; 2 nguoi chap nhan -> dich chuyen vao san duel (khu rieng), hoi day mau, khoa pet-tool.
- Luat: 1v1 (hero cua minh), thang thi nhan **Duel Point**; sau tran tra lai vi tri + hoi mau, khong mat do/exp.
- Cuoc vang tuy chon (0 / 500 / 2000), bang xep hang `-rank`.
- Che do **Duel 3v3 team** (doi 3 Pokemon: mua them hero phu) - de sau.
- Mua phan thuong bang Duel Point: skin, TM hiem, Rare Candy.

## D. Can bang + tien trinh
- Level cap theo huy hieu (0 huy hieu = lv 30, ...) de hang nguoi choi khong vuot qua gym.
- Quai hoang da: ty le rot item theo tier; Elite Four moi (4 phong), Champion.
- Mission hang ngay (giet X quai, thang 1 gym) cho vang/Rare Candy.
- Legend: moi legend co **phong + puzzle** (da ghi trong ideas_for_approval.md).
- Tut/lenh: `-help`, bang goi y nhanh o nha Pokemon List.

## E. De xuat thu tu lam
1. Huy hieu gym + level cap + Elite Four (dinh huong)
2. Held item + item tien hoa
3. Duel 1v1 + rank
4. Mission hang ngay + legend puzzle
